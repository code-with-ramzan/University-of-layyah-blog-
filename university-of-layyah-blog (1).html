About the University of Layyah

The University of Layyah began in 2009 as the Bahadur Sub-Campus of Bahauddin Zakariya University and was chartered as an independent university under the University of Layyah Act 2022. It is recognised by the Higher Education Commission (HEC) of Pakistan and serves students across Layyah and southern Punjab.

Official portal: ul.edu.pk
